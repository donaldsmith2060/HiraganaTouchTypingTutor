Hiragana Touch Typing Tutor is an interactive app designed to help you master Japanese Kana touch typing on Android.

Features:
- Practice Hiragana, Katakana, and Romaji characters side-by-side.
- Real-time native Text-to-Speech (TTS) audio pronunciation for Japanese characters.
- Works with both touch screen software keyboards and physical hardware keyboards.
- Track completed characters and build typing speed and accuracy.
- Lightweight and privacy-friendly with no ads or tracking.
